# Sensor Architecture

## Environmental Sensing

The device integrates air quality sensing components for monitoring environmental conditions.

Measured parameters include:
- particulate matter,
- airborne contaminants,
- airflow characteristics,
- environmental quality indicators.

---

## Sensor Integration

Sensors communicate with the embedded controller for:
- real-time acquisition,
- processing,
- visualization,
- and monitoring.

---

## Design Goals

Key goals included:
- portability,
- low power operation,
- reliable sensing,
- compact integration,
- and airflow optimization.
