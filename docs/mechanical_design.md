# Mechanical Design

## Enclosure Development

The enclosure was designed using iterative CAD modeling and prototyping.

Design goals included:
- portability,
- structural integrity,
- airflow optimization,
- sensor accessibility,
- and compact packaging.

---

## Design Iterations

Multiple enclosure revisions were developed during the project.

Mechanical features include:
- removable lid,
- internal component mounting,
- airflow channels,
- battery compartment,
- and fan integration.

---

## Fabrication

The enclosure components were fabricated using 3D printing techniques and assembled manually.
