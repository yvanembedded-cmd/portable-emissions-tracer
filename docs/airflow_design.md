# Airflow Design

## Overview

Proper airflow management was critical for ensuring accurate environmental sensing and thermal stability.

The enclosure was designed to:
- guide airflow across sensors,
- improve sampling consistency,
- and maintain stable internal airflow conditions.

---

## Fan Integration

A compact cooling fan was integrated into the enclosure to:
- maintain continuous airflow,
- improve sensor exposure,
- and reduce stagnant air regions.

---

## Airflow Analysis

Airflow simulations and enclosure iterations were evaluated during the mechanical design process.

Multiple enclosure revisions were tested to optimize:
- airflow paths,
- sensor positioning,
- and internal spacing.
